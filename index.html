What is AI, really?

No tech jargon. Just plain language.

In one sentence: AI is software trained on huge amounts of data so it can recognize patterns and use them to answer questions, generate content, or complete tasks, similar to how a person learns from experience, but much faster and at a much bigger scale. Today's AI is "narrow" AI: very good at specific tasks, not a general thinking machine like in movies.

AI is a very smart assistant

Think of it like a colleague who has read every book, every report, and every manual — and can answer your questions instantly. You still make the decisions; it helps you get there faster.

Everyday

AI helps you do your job better

In architecture and design, AI can generate concept sketches, check drawings for errors, write meeting summaries, and compare documents — so you spend time on creative thinking, not repetitive tasks.

Which AI tool should I use?

A simple guide based on your role and task type. Always follow CPG data security rules.

Task Recommended Tool Who can use Data Safety Level
Writing emails, reports, summaries Microsoft Copilot All staff Internal OK
Research & general questions ChatGPT All staff No client data
Concept renders & mood boards Midjourney / Veras Architects, Designers Public images only
BIM model checking Autodesk AI Technical team Secure sandbox
Scripting & automation Claude / Copilot Advanced users Internal OK
Data analysis & dashboards Excel Copilot Managers, QS Internal OK
Important: Never paste confidential project data, client names, or financial figures into public AI tools like ChatGPT or Midjourney. Use Microsoft Copilot (connected to your O365 account) for internal data — it's secured by CPG's IT policies.
