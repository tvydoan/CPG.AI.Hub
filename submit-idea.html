What is AI, really?

No tech jargon. Just plain language.

In one sentence: AI stands for artificial intelligence, a field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, generating content, making predictions, and supporting decisions. Most AI used today is narrow AI, designed for specific tasks rather than general human-like thinking.

AI is a very smart assistant

Think of it like a colleague who has read every book, every report, and every manual , and can answer your questions instantly. You still make the decisions; it helps you get there faster.

Everyday

AI helps you do your job better

In architecture and design, AI can generate concept sketches, check drawings for errors, write meeting summaries, and compare documents , so you spend time on creative thinking, not repetitive tasks.

AI Workshop Slide Outline

12-slide keynote outline for Soh Wee Keong's opening at the AI Consulting Workshop:

Use AI safely at CPG

Know what information you can share, what needs a second look, and what should never go into a public AI tool.

SAFE TO USE
  • Public or general information
  • Published references and precedent research
  • Drafts with no client or project names
  • Public images for style and mood direction
CHECK FIRST
  • Internal CPG documents and processes
  • Notes that reference a project internally
  • Anything you're not sure is confidential
  • Ask your manager before pasting it in
DO NOT UPLOAD
  • Confidential client data or client names
  • Financial figures, contracts, BOQs
  • BIM models or drawings outside the approved sandbox
  • Anything covered by an NDA

Rule of thumb: Microsoft Copilot is connected to your O365 account and safe for internal CPG data. Public tools like ChatGPT, Midjourney, and Veras should never see confidential project or client details.

Data Safety & AI Rules

Guidelines for CPG Vietnam staff on using AI tools safely in daily operations.

01

Do NOT upload

  • Confidential client data or client names
  • Financial figures, contracts, or BOQs
  • BIM models or drawings outside approved sandboxes
  • Anything subject to an NDA
02

Check first

  • Internal CPG process documents
  • Notes that reference internal project names
  • Anything where confidentiality is uncertain
  • Ask your department lead before pasting
03

Safe to use

  • Public or general industry information
  • Published references and precedent research
  • Draft text with all project & client names removed
  • Public images for style and mood direction

Rule of thumb: Microsoft Copilot is connected to your O365 account and safe for internal CPG data. Public tools like ChatGPT, Midjourney, and Veras must never receive confidential project or client details.
